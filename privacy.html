Privacy policy

Hisaab collects no data. Last updated 9 November 2026.

What stays on your phone

  • Bills. Items, people, UPI IDs and payments are saved only on your iPhone.
  • Receipt photos. Hisaab reads them on the phone with Apple's Vision framework and doesn't keep or send them.
  • Voice input. Speech is turned into text on the phone. Audio isn't recorded or sent.
  • Apple Intelligence. On supported iPhones, Hisaab uses Apple's on-device model to sort items. Nothing leaves the phone.

Friends at the table

When you let friends join a bill, Hisaab shares that bill directly with their phones over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, encrypted by iOS. It doesn't go through any server.

Payments

Hisaab makes UPI QR codes and opens UPI apps with the amount filled in. The payment happens in your UPI app, between you and your friend. Hisaab never sees or handles it.

No tracking

Hisaab has no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code that collects data.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
