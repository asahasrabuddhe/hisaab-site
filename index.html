Common questions

Does Hisaab send my bills anywhere?

No. Bills, photos and voice input stay on your phone. Hisaab has no account and no server.

Does Hisaab handle money?

No. The UPI QR codes and pay links ask your UPI app to pay the person who covered the bill directly. Hisaab never holds or routes money.

How do friends join a bill at the table?

Open the bill, tap the menu, then "Let friends pick". Friends tap Join on their own phones. Both phones need Wi-Fi or Bluetooth turned on and Hisaab allowed on the local network.

My UPI app warns about the payment request

Some UPI apps warn when a request comes with the amount filled in. Check the name and UPI ID match your friend before you pay.
